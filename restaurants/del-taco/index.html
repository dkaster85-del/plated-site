Del Taco macros

Del Taco publishes nutrition for 166 menu items and options, all listed below with calories, protein, carbs and fat. The most protein in a single item is the Epic Carne Asada Steak Cali Bacon Burrito, 414g, with 51 g for 1,050 calories. Figures are Del Taco’s own published nutrition information (source), collected October 8, 2026. Recipes, portions and regional menus vary, so check with the restaurant if you need exact numbers.
