Captain D's macros

Captain D's publishes nutrition for 63 menu items and options, all listed below with calories, protein, carbs and fat. The most protein in a single item is the Supreme Sampler with 54 g for 1,140 calories. Figures are Captain D's’s own published nutrition information (source), collected October 8, 2026. Recipes, portions and regional menus vary, so check with the restaurant if you need exact numbers.
