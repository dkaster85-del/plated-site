TGI Fridays macros

TGI Fridays publishes nutrition for 226 menu items and options, all listed below with calories, protein, carbs and fat. The most protein in a single item is the Cajun Shrimp & Chicken Pasta with Breadstick with 81 g for 1,410 calories. For the most protein per calorie, the Grilled Chicken Breast (1) (ADD-ON A PROTEIN) gets 62% of its calories from protein.

Figures are TGI Fridays’s own published nutrition information (source), collected October 8, 2026. Recipes, portions and regional menus vary, so check with the restaurant if you need exact numbers.
