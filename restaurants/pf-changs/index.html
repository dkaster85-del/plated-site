P.F. Chang's macros

P.F. Chang's publishes nutrition for 242 menu items and options, all listed below with calories, protein, carbs and fat. The most protein in a single item is the GF Chang's Spicy Chicken (Gluten) with 78 g for 1,160 calories. For the most protein per calorie, the Chicken with Broccoli (Main Entrées / Traditional Sized) gets 50% of its calories from protein.

Figures are P.F. Chang's’s own published nutrition information (source), collected October 8, 2026. Recipes, portions and regional menus vary, so check with the restaurant if you need exact numbers.
