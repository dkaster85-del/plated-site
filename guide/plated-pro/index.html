How to use Plated  /  Plated Pro

Plated Pro

Everything you need to log food, workouts, runs and bodyweight is free. Plated Pro is a monthly subscription ($9.99 in the US; the upgrade screen shows your local price). You buy it in the iPhone app, and it works wherever you sign in.

Subscribe and manage

Subscribe to Plated Pro iPhone only#

  1. Go to Profile → ☰ → Settings and tap Upgrade to Plated Pro. Or tap anything with a PRO badge.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the upgrade screen.
  3. Tap Subscribe and confirm with Apple.
  4. Pro switches on straight away. Ads disappear and the PRO badges go.

Restore Pro on a new phone#

  1. Sign in to Plated with the same account.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases, at the bottom of the upgrade screen or in Settings → Subscription.
  3. You’ll see “Purchases restored.” If not, check you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you paid with.

Manage or cancel#

  1. Go to Profile → ☰ → Settings → Subscription.
  2. Tap Manage or cancel. Apple’s subscription page opens.
  3. Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

TipDeleting the app doesn’t cancel the subscription. You can also cancel in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Use Pro on the web and other devices#

  1. Pro belongs to your Plated account, not your phone. Sign in anywhere and it’s there.
  2. On the web, there’s no Subscribe button. Subscribe in the iPhone app, and Pro is active on the web the next time you sign in.
  3. If Pro lapses, your Pro settings (like rest-day targets) are kept, but switch off until you subscribe again.
