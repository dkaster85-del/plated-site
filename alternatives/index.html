One app instead of a food tracker and a workout log

A lot of people who lift and count macros run two or three apps: a food tracker such as MyFitnessPal, a workout log such as Hevy or Strong, and something else for runs. Plated does all three in one free iPhone app, and adds a feed for sharing your progress with friends.

Switching from Hevy or Strong

Export your workouts as a CSV from Hevy or Strong and import the file into Plated. It matches each exercise to its own library and shows you any match it isn’t sure of before saving, so your PRs and progress charts carry over without splitting one lift into two.

Common questions

Is Plated free?

Yes. Food tracking, barcode scanning, the workout log, GPS runs and the feed are free, with small banner ads. Plated Pro is $9.99 a month in the US, with a 7-day free trial for eligible accounts. It removes the ads and adds AI label scanning, training analytics, progressive overload suggestions and comparing with friends.
