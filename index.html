What's inside

Five tabs, and none of them are filler

Plated is built around the things you do every day, not around a dashboard you check once and forget. Each tab does one job properly.

Nutrition

Logging that doesn't fight you

The fastest path from a package in your hand to a number in your day.

  • Barcode scanning against Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central
  • AI label scanner — photograph a nutrition label and it fills itself in, serving size included
  • Recipes and meals you build once and log in a tap
  • Fill My Macros suggests dinner based on what you have left
Train

Every set, and what to do next

A workout log that reads your history instead of just storing it.

  • Progressive overload suggestions anchored to your best over 90 days, not your last bad night
  • Full exercise library, editable, plus your own custom movements
  • Heart rate live from a Bluetooth strap or Apple Watch
  • Import from Hevy so you don't start from zero
Progress

Proof it's working

The part that keeps you going when the mirror is being unhelpful.

  • GPS run tracking with route, distance, pace and elevation
  • Muscle heatmap showing what you've actually trained
  • Weight and measurements charted over time
  • Apple Health sync, both directions
Feed

People doing the same thing

Small and accountable, not another place to perform.

  • Share workouts and meals with photos or video
  • Follow friends, comment, and keep each other honest
  • Public or private — your profile, your call
  • Report and block on everything, enforced both ways

One honest note

Plated is a tracking tool, not a doctor

Calorie targets, macro splits and overload suggestions are estimates generated from formulas and the numbers you enter. They are not medical or nutritional advice. Talk to a physician or a registered dietitian before changing your diet or training — particularly if you are pregnant, under 18, or managing any medical condition or eating disorder.
