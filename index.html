What's in the app

Every picture below is a screen from the app. Most of it is free, and anything that needs Pro says so.

Nutrition

Scan it and it's logged

Scan a barcode and the food is in your day. With Pro, take a photo of the nutrition label instead and Plated fills in the food, serving size included. It reads labels in any language.

Also here: recipes and saved meals, restaurant menus, and with Pro, Fill My Macros, which suggests dinner from what you have left.

Train

It tells you the next weight

Before each exercise, Pro suggests a weight and reps from your best sets over the last 90 days. Tick the set off and the timer and volume keep running.

Also here: an exercise library you can edit, GPS runs with route and pace, live heart rate from a strap or Apple Watch (Pro), and import from another app's CSV file.

Progress

See which muscles you trained

The heatmap shades each muscle by how much work it got over 7, 14 or 30 days. Tap one for its numbers. It comes with Pro.

Also here: a workout calendar, bodyweight and measurements, Apple Health sync in both directions, and with Pro, a chart for every exercise.

Feed

Post the workout

Share a workout, a meal or a run with a photo or video. Friends see every lift, and with Pro they can save the workout to try it themselves.

Also here: PR badges, stickers for your Instagram story, private profiles, and report and block on every post.

One honest note

Plated is a tracking tool, not a doctor

Calorie targets, macro splits and overload suggestions are estimates generated from formulas and the numbers you enter. They are not medical or nutritional advice. Talk to a physician or a registered dietitian before changing your diet or training — particularly if you are pregnant, under 18, or managing any medical condition or eating disorder.
